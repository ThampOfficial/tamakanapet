Pick what fits your cat

Some cats are happiest in their own home. Others do better with company around all day. We offer both, so you can choose based on your cat's personality — not just what's convenient for us.

Drop-in visits at your home

Best for shy cats or cats who don't do well with a change of environment. We come to you at the times you book — feed, fresh water, litter, and playtime — all in familiar surroundings.

Choose 20–30 min or 1 hour per visit · flexible time windows
  • Feeding on your cat's usual schedule
  • Litter box scooped and cleaned
  • 10–15 minutes of play or cuddles
  • Photo sent after every visit

Boarding at our home

Best for social cats, or if you'd rather not have someone coming into your place while you're away. Your cat gets their own space in a real home, with someone around all day — not a kennel.

1-night minimum · limited cats per stay so everyone gets attention
  • Separate space per household — no forced mixing
  • Comfortable giving vet-prescribed medication
  • Daily photo or video update
  • Pickup/drop-off available nearby
